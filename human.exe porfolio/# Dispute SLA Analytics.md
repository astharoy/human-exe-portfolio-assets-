# Dispute SLA Analytics

### Analysing SLA Compliance, Case Ageing and Documentation Gaps

**Project Type:** Data Analytics
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, SQL
**Dataset:** 2,770 cleaned records (synthetic dispute dataset)
**Status:** Completed Analysis

### 1. Business Problem

The objective was to evaluate dispute-handling performance by analysing SLA compliance, identifying overdue cases, and understanding factors associated with delays.

The analysis focused on three questions:

* Are dispute cases being resolved within their defined SLA timelines?
* Where are delays and SLA breaches occurring?
* Is documentation completeness associated with SLA performance?

### 2. Approach

I cleaned and analysed 2,770 records using Excel and SQL.

The analysis included:

* Case ageing and status analysis.
* SLA breach identification.
* SLA compliance calculations.
* Comparison of breach patterns based on documentation completeness.
* Separate consideration of open and closed cases.

### 3. Key Findings

**91.2% of closed cases were within SLA.**

The analysis also identified:

* Incomplete documentation was associated with a higher SLA breach rate.
* Open cases required separate monitoring because they had not yet reached a final resolution.
* Cases that had exceeded their deadlines needed further investigation to understand the reasons for delays.

### 4. Business Implications

The findings suggest that improving documentation completeness could help reduce operational delays.

Further investigation into open cases, workload distribution and recurring delay reasons would help management identify opportunities to improve dispute resolution performance.
